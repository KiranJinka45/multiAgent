## Environment Guidelines
- **Constrained**: Low CPU (1-2 cores), Low RAM (1-2GB).
- **Ephemeral**: Fresh VM/Container for every session.
- **Disconnected**: Limited outbound connectivity (simulated).

## Success Metrics
- **Zero-Support Completion**: Operator finishes primary playbook without asking questions.
- **MTTR (Mean Time To Recovery)**: Time from incident trigger to "Healthy" status.
- **Friction Density**: Number of `ztanctl friction` logs per hour.
