# Context: Phase 13.3R — Governance Legibility & Constitutional Compression

## Objective
Reduce governance complexity and improve institutional clarity. Ensure all stabilization actions are explainable to operators and auditable by institutions without requiring "creator interpretation."

## Requirements
- **Governance Compression**:
    - `packages/governance-compression/`: Simplify telemetry and generate institutional summaries.
- **Constitutional Hierarchy**:
    - `packages/governance-constitution/`: Map rule precedence and emergency escalation hierarchy.
- **Explainability Engine**:
    - `packages/governance-explainability/`: Generate narratives for why containment triggered and its constitutional justification.
- **CLI (ztanctl)**:
    - `governance explain/summary`
    - `constitution inspect`

## Strategic Intent
Transform complex economic stabilization mechanics into legible institutional summaries with expandable forensic detail. Prioritize institutional clarity over technical expansion.

## Recommended Approach
Expandable detail: Simplified institutional summaries by default, with expandable forensic reasoning chains for audit.
