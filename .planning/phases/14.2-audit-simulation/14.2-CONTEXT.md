# Context: Phase 14.2 — Independent Governance Audit Simulation

## Objective
Validate that external institutional auditors can independently reconstruct, interpret, and verify ZTAN governance events without creator involvement. Prove "trust portability" and "audit survivability."

## Requirements
- **Audit Simulator**:
    - `packages/audit-simulations/`: Replay governance incidents and verify constitutional authority independently.
- **Evidence Pack Generator**:
    - `packages/audit-evidence/`: Generate constrained, institutionally-legible evidence packs (Merkle bundles, decision summaries).
- **Audit Consistency Engine**:
    - `packages/audit-consistency/`: Measure interpretation divergence between multiple auditors to ensure deterministic trust.
- **Third-Party CLI**:
    - `ztanctl audit replay/lineage/verify/summarize`.

## Strategic Intent
Move from "internal correctness" to "external verifiability." The system must survive an audit by an organization that has zero "tribal knowledge" of the project's history.

## Success Criteria
- External auditors independently reach the same conclusions as the system.
- Replay lineage is 100% cryptographically provable from evidence packs.
- Audit consistency scores > 95%.
