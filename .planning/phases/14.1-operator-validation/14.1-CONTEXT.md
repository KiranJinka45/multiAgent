# Context: Phase 14.1 — External Operator Validation

## Objective
Validate that external institutional operators can correctly interpret and respond to governance events without internal project context. Move from "technical correctness" to "operational comprehensibility."

## Requirements
- **Operator Simulations**:
    - `packages/operator-simulations/`: Automated "Confusion Scenarios" where the system behaves correctly but requires human interpretation.
- **Onboarding Manual**:
    - `docs/OPERATOR_ONBOARDING_MANUAL.md`: A concise guide for new operators to understand the ZTAN constitutional hierarchy.
- **CLI Validation**:
    - `ztanctl operator test`: Trigger simulated "soft failures" or "containment events" to test operator response time and accuracy.

## Strategic Intent
Testing the "human interface" of the institution. If an operator cannot explain *why* the circuit breaker tripped within 2 minutes of using the CLI, the governance legibility has failed.

## Success Criteria
- 100% accuracy in incident interpretation during simulations.
- < 5 minute onboarding time for a technically literate non-creator.
- Operator confidence scores > 90% during stress scenarios.
