# Context: Phase 19.1 — Constitutional Diversity Boundaries & Safe Disagreement

## Objective
Enable federations to diverge safely, maintaining bounded constitutional diversity while preserving interoperability and trust continuity. Transform "semantic convergence" into "survivable constitutional pluralism."

## Requirements
- **Diversity Boundary Engine**:
    - `packages/diversity-boundaries/`: Define, monitor, and enforce the limits of safe constitutional divergence.
- **Disagreement Resolution Protocol**:
    - `packages/disagreement-protocol/`: Manage trust continuity during policy interpretation disputes without requiring immediate convergence.
- **Pluralistic Interoperability Layer**:
    - `packages/pluralistic-interop/`: Ensure technical interoperability under differing governance philosophies.
- **CLI (ztanctl)**:
    - `federation diversity-status/dispute/pluralism-check`.

## Strategic Intent
Avoid "civilizational ossification" caused by over-convergence. Healthy civilizations require shared invariants AND bounded diversity. This phase ensures ZTAN can survive ideological evolution and disagreement without fragmentation.

## Success Criteria
- Independent federations can maintain divergent governance policies within safe constitutional boundaries.
- Disagreements on policy interpretation do not cause immediate trust discontinuity.
- The network remains technically and institutionally interoperable under differing governance philosophies.
- Pluralistic resilience is validated through simulated ideological fragmentation.
