# Context: Phase 14.3 — Institutional Deployment Compression

## Objective
Reduce institutional onboarding friction and operational deployment complexity. Transform ZTAN from "technically mature" to "operationally adoptable."

## Requirements
- **Institutional Profiles**:
    - `configs/institutional-profiles/`: Preconfigured defaults for Finance, Sovereignty, and Enterprise.
- **Deployment Compression Engine**:
    - `packages/deployment-compression/`: Automate safe deployment generation and governance-safe defaults.
- **Readiness Validator**:
    - `packages/readiness-validator/`: verify governance integrity and containment readiness before activation.
- **CLI (ztanctl)**:
    - `deploy init/validate/certify/explain`.
- **Guided Onboarding**:
    - `packages/institutional-onboarding/`: Guided walkthroughs and operational role mapping.

## Strategic Intent
"Usability is part of security." If an institution deploys incorrectly or misinterprets governance during onboarding, the system is operationally unsafe. We must optimize for low-friction, opinionated defaults.

## Success Criteria
- Deployment time < 10 minutes for a non-author operator.
- 0% configuration-induced governance failures during onboarding.
- Institutional trust increases during the deployment process.
