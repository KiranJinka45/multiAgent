# Context: Phase 24.1 — Institutional Fatigue Detection & Ritual Integrity

## Objective
Ensure ZTAN institutions remain operationally disciplined and cognitively engaged over decadal time horizons. Transform institutional survivability into **long-duration operational discipline survivability** by detecting operator fatigue and preserving the integrity of governance rituals.

## Requirements
- **Operator Fatigue Monitor**:
    - `packages/operator-fatigue-monitor/`: Analyze operator behavior patterns to detect declining attentiveness, procedural shortcutting, and verification fatigue.
- **Ritual Integrity Engine**:
    - `packages/ritual-integrity/`: Validate that governance rituals (signing, auditing, consensus checks) remain meaningful and rigorously executed rather than symbolic bureaucracy.
- **Complacency Stressor**:
    - `packages/complacency-stressor/`: Inject unexpected verification prompts or "monotony breaks" to test institutional alertness during long stable periods.
- **Sociological Telemetry Indexer**:
    - `packages/sociological-telemetry/`: Track long-term institutional health metrics: "Audit Ritual Decay," "Over-Trust Index," and "Organizational Drift."
- **CLI (ztanctl sociology)**:
    - New suite of commands: `sociology fatigue-check`, `sociology ritual-audit`, `sociology drift-index`.

## Strategic Intent
Pivot from "Empirical Exposure" to **Long-Duration Operational Sociology**. ZTAN's ultimate challenge is resisting the entropy of human institutions—boredom, complacency, and the decay of discipline. This phase introduces the sensors and stressors necessary to maintain civilizational-scale institutional rigor over decades.

## Success Criteria
- Declining operator attentiveness is accurately detected and reported.
- Governance rituals are verified for procedural integrity and semantic engagement.
- Institutional alertness is maintained through complacency stressors.
- Sociological telemetry provides measurable proof of long-term operational discipline.
