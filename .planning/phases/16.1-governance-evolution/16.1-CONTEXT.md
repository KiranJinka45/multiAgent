# Context: Phase 16.1 — Bounded Constitutional Mutation

## Objective
Implement the mechanism for safe, replayable constitutional policy upgrades, allowing institutions to evolve governance without breaking historical continuity or trust history.

## Requirements
- **Evolution Engine**:
    - `packages/governance-evolution/`: Manage policy upgrades and constitutional mutations.
- **Continuity Migration**:
    - `packages/continuity-migration/`: Bridge the replay gap between old and new policies.
- **Upgrade Replay Engine**:
    - `packages/upgrade-replay/`: Verify that upgrades were performed constitutionally and deterministically.
- **CLI (ztanctl)**:
    - `governance propose/upgrade/migrate`.

## Strategic Intent
Avoid "institutional sclerosis" by allowing controlled, replayable evolution. Ensure that ZTAN remains adaptive while preserving its core "monotony" and "continuity" moats.

## Success Criteria
- Policy upgrades are performed without breaking the governance lineage.
- All historical events remain replayable under their original policy context.
- The transition between old and new policies is cryptographically anchored.
- Federated peers can verify the legitimacy of a peer's constitutional evolution.
