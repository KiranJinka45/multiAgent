# Context: Phase 15.3 — Federated Trust Containment & Reputation Quarantine

## Objective
Prevent compromised, degraded, or adversarial federations from destabilizing the broader institutional trust network while preserving sovereign independence and replayable legitimacy.

## Requirements
- **Federated Trust Containment Engine**:
    - `packages/federated-containment/`: Detect trust anomalies and isolate degraded continuity histories.
- **Reputation Quarantine Layer**:
    - `packages/reputation-quarantine/`: Manage staged trust degradation and track recovery eligibility.
- **Federated Recovery Engine**:
    - `packages/federated-recovery/`: Rebuild trust continuity and re-certify institutional legitimacy post-incident.
- **Cross-Federation Drift Analysis**:
    - `packages/federated-drift/`: Detect hidden policy drift or anomalous replay behavior across federation boundaries.
- **CLI (ztanctl)**:
    - `federation quarantine/downgrade/recover/drift`.

## Strategic Intent
Transform "trust interoperability" into "federated trust containment resilience." Ensure that the failure of one institution does not poison the trust history of the entire network.

## Success Criteria
- Degraded federations are successfully quarantined with 0% contagion.
- Trust recovery is possible through verifiable post-incident replay.
- Quarantine actions remain replayable and constitutionally bounded.
- Sovereign trust interoperability survives the compromise of a peer.
