# Phase 08.6 Context: Constitutional Locking & Operational Sealing

## Strategic Pillars
Phase 8.6 is the "Sealing" phase. We are transitioning ZTAN from a software platform into a **Time-Anchored Operational Trust Infrastructure**. This phase is not about adding features; it is about certifying invariants and removing the "Founder Dependency."

## Core Objectives

### 1. Protocol Specification V1.0 (The Institutional Contract)
- **Goal:** Formally define the schemas and semantics that govern ZTAN truth.
- **Output:** A formal specification defining Evidence Packets, Epoch Semantics, Replay Invariants, and Governance Rotation rules.

### 2. Invariant Lock Audit (The Safety Guarantee)
- **Goal:** Certify that the system is physically incapable of violating its own governance rules.
- **Verification:** Ensure no hidden recursive cognition, uncontrolled mutation paths, or cross-tenant write vectors exist in the frozen core.

### 3. Baseline Performance Certification (Operational Predictability)
- **Goal:** Document predictable operational latencies (Replay, Rollback, Recovery).
- **Metric:** Predictability over throughput.

### 4. Non-Author Operability Test (Clean-Room Drill)
- **Goal:** Prove the system can be operated by a non-creator.
- **Success Criteria:** A "Fresh Operator" can perform a cell recovery and governance rotation using only the baseline CLI and specification.

### 5. Permanent Reduction Pass (Strategic Simplification)
- **Goal:** Strip away dead abstractions and unused extension points.
- **Rule:** The system survives because it is smaller than its ambition, not larger.

## The Final Moat: Clean Operational History
The ultimate value of ZTAN is not its current capability, but its **Accumulated Operational History**. Phase 8.6 ensures that this history remains verifiable, stable, and survivable for the next century of institutional operation.
