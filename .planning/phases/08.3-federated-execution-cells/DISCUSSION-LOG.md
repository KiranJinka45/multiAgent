# Discussion Log: Phase 08.3 Cellular Survivability

**Date:** 2026-05-10
**Participants:** USER, Antigravity (the agent)

## Context
Phase 8.3 addresses the "most dangerous scaling step": federation. The discussion established strict boundaries to prevent "distributed complexity collapse" and prioritize "boring survivability."

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Federation of Trust vs. Cognition
- **Decision:** Share only signed assets (evidence, roots, attestations). Never share live cognition, memory, or execution state.
- **Rationale:** Shared cognition creates cross-cell dependencies that threaten institutional stability and regional containment.

### 2. Cellular Sovereignty
- **Decision:** Each cell must be a fully independent fortress.
- **Rationale:** Independence is the only guarantee of regional failure containment. A cell must operate even when the global mesh is fragmented.

### 3. Trust Portability
- **Decision:** Evidence packets must be detachably verifiable across cells.
- **Rationale:** Trust must move with the mission artifacts, not depend on a central database.

### 4. Prohibition of T5 in Federation
- **Decision:** Maintain the strict ban on systemic/cross-tenant operations, even in federated models.
- **Rationale:** Preserves localized failure containment.

### 5. Managing "Evidence Inflation"
- **Decision:** Prioritize "Maximal Forensic Density, Minimal Cognitive Load."
- **Rationale:** Prevents auditor paralysis and governance erosion via review fatigue.

## Conclusion
The objective of Phase 8.3 is to scale the "Survival Radius" of the ZTAN platform without increasing its "Complexity Surface." We are building a federation of sovereign, trustworthy cells.
