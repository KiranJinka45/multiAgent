# Phase 45: Passive Operational Observation & Selective Reliability Refinement

## Objective
Transition Nexus ZTAN into long-duration quiet operation through passive observation and evidence-driven refinements. Ensure the platform remains stable, maintenance-sustainable, and cognitively simple over years of operation.

## Waves

### Wave 1: Observation & Monitoring Alignment (OBSERVE)
Align existing monitoring tools with the "Passive Observation" directive.
- [ ] **OBSERVE-01**: Review `drift-analyzer.ts` and `process-friction.ts` to ensure they capture long-term trends.
- [ ] **OBSERVE-02**: Verify that the `continuous-validation-engine.ts` operates in a "quiet" mode (passive monitoring vs active injection).
- [ ] **OBSERVE-03**: Audit `METRICS.json` for redundant telemetry that can be pruned to reduce maintenance burden.

### Wave 2: Longitudinal Reporting Infrastructure (REPORT)
Enhance reporting to cover all requested longitudinal deliverables.
- [ ] **REPORT-01**: Implement `scripts/generate-refinement-report.ts` to track evidence-triggered changes.
- [ ] **REPORT-02**: Update `scripts/generate-stewardship-reports.ts` to include specific "Replay Sustainability" and "Operational Diversity" deep-dives.
- [ ] **REPORT-03**: Verify that reports are written to `reports/stewardship/` for long-term persistence.

### Wave 3: Selective Reliability Refinement (REFINE)
Execute refinements based on operational evidence (e.g., reducing friction or simplifying workflows).
- [ ] **REFINE-01**: Simplify the `DASHBOARD.html` to improve readability for junior SREs.
- [ ] **REFINE-02**: Prune legacy operational artifacts and stale tooling identified during observation.
- [ ] **REFINE-03**: Optimize replay navigation for long historical ledgers.

### Wave 4: Certification & Handover (STEWARD)
Finalize the stewardship baseline.
- [ ] **STEWARD-01**: Generate the full suite of Phase 45 Stewardship Reports.
- [ ] **STEWARD-02**: Update `STEWARDSHIP_CHARTER.md` with lessons learned from longitudinal observation.
- [ ] **STEWARD-03**: Perform a final "Operational Calmness" audit.

## Success Criteria
- 100% of required deliverables (Reliability, Replay, Maintenance, Diversity, Refinement reports) generated.
- Maintenance burden reduced or stabilized (target < 3h/week).
- Replay remains readable across 210+ days of operational history.
- Zero new architectural components introduced.
- Dashboard comprehension score for junior SREs at 100%.
