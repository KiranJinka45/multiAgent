# Phase 08.5 Context: Disaster Recovery & Institutional Survivability Drills

## Operational Intent
Phase 8.5 is the final stress-test of the ZTAN institutional substrate. Having established time-survivable trust roots in Phase 8.4, we must now prove that the institution remains legitimate and functional during real operational degradation. This is the "Boringly Reliable" final exam.

## Core Objectives: Institutional Survivability

### 1. Full Cell Loss Recovery (Resurrection)
- **Objective:** Prove a cell can be destroyed and reconstructed without breaking trust lineage.
- **Drill:** Perform a "Cold Start" recovery where a cell is wiped and resurrected using only local backups and the Trust Bridge root.
- **Success Criteria:** The resurrected cell can successfully verify its own historical Evidence Packets.

### 2. Governance Succession & Stewardship rotation
- **Objective:** Prove the system survives the loss or rotation of its original operators (Founder Independence).
- **Drill:** Rotate all governance signers and operators.
- **Success Criteria:** The new operator set can finalize missions and anchor roots without privileged "backdoors" or historical secret knowledge.

### 3. Historical Archive Reconstruction
- **Objective:** Ensure historical memory is reconstructable from raw evidence.
- **Drill:** Purge all runtime caches and reconstruct the Governance Merkle Tree from the `InstitutionalEvidencePacket` corpus.
- **Success Criteria:** The reconstruction matches the latest root signed by the current epoch.

### 4. Long-Duration Entropy Validation
- **Objective:** Detect silent drift in determinism or process erosion.
- **Drill:** Run a continuous operational window (simulated 90-day window) with heavy replay and audit pressure.
- **Success Criteria:** No drift in blast-radius manifests or trust score calculation across the duration.

### 5. Operator Simplicity Audit
- **Objective:** Ensure the system can be operated by non-authors.
- **Success Criteria:** Documentation and CLI tools are sufficient for a "Fresh Operator" to perform recovery and audit tasks without assistance.

## Strategic Guardrail: No New Invention
- **Rule:** Phase 8.5 MUST NOT add new core features.
- **Goal:** Hardening, Documentation, and Recovery Procedure finalization.

## Success Metric: Institutional Legitimacy
ZTAN is deemed successful when it demonstrates that it can lose a region, rotate its entire staff, and still produce incontrovertible forensic evidence of its internal operations.
