# Context: Phase 13.2 — Dynamic Slashing-as-a-Service

## Objective
Enable cross-federation slashing markets to outsource governance enforcement and ensure direct economic penalties for institutional breaches.

## Requirements
- **Integration**: Connect `SlashingEngine` to `StakeManager`.
- **Economic Penalties**:
    - Implement `StakeManager.slash(fedId: string, amount: number)` to reduce collateral.
    - Implement `SlashingMarket`: A registry where verified reports earn "Bounty Rewards" from the slashed collateral.
- **CLI (ztanctl)**:
    - `slash report --fed <ID> --type <TYPE> --evidence <JSON>`
    - `slash status --fed <ID>`
- **Verification**:
    - Simulation of Equivocation and automated slashing.
    - Bounty payout verification.

## Current State
- `SlashingEngine` exists but is isolated from `StakeManager`.
- `StakeManager` handles deposits and unbonding but has no "slash" primitive.
- No CLI commands for slashing.
