# Phase 08.4 Context: Trust Root Hardening & Operability

## Operational Intent
Phase 8.4 focuses on the long-term integrity and continuity of the institutional trust rooted in ZTAN. Having established independent cellular sovereignty, we must now ensure that the cryptographic foundations (Trust Roots) are survivable across key rotations, governance shifts, and regional compromises.

## Core Objectives: Trust Continuity

### 1. Key Rotation & Revocation Policy
- **Objective:** Prevent "Trust Entropy" by implementing survivable key rotation semantics.
- **Rule:** Cells MUST support rolling their local attestation keys without invalidating historically signed evidence packets.
- **Rule:** Implement a "Revocation List" primitive that allows the Trust Bridge to propagate cell-level key compromises across the federation.

### 2. Governance Root Versioning (Epochs)
- **Objective:** Ensure replay continuity across institutional governance transitions.
- **Rule:** Every `InstitutionalEvidencePacket` must be anchored to a specific `GovernanceEpoch`.
- **Rule:** The Witness Server must maintain a historical archive of Governance Merkle Roots to allow post-facto verification of missions from previous epochs.

### 3. Offline Verification Tooling (Institutional Independence)
- **Objective:** Enable independent auditability without requiring a live ZTAN network.
- **Rule:** Develop the `ztan-auditor` CLI—a detached verification tool that uses local policy files and public cell-keys to validate Evidence Packets.
- **Rule:** This tool must be the "Final Validator" for institutional truth, independent of the orchestration substrate.

### 4. Deployment Reproducibility & Recovery
- **Objective:** Hardening the "Boringly Reliable" deployment discipline.
- **Rule:** Formalize the cellular recovery drills (Cell Resurrection) to prove that a cell can be restored to a trusted state from local immutable backups.

## Strategic Guardrail: No Expanded Autonomy
- **Rule:** Phase 8.4 MUST NOT introduce new cognitive capabilities, orchestration layers, or autonomous scaling logic.
- **Goal:** Hardening existing foundations to ensure they survive long-duration enterprise validation.

## Success Metric: Institutional Finality
The platform is deemed successful when an external auditor can take a raw `.evidence.json` file and verify its total integrity (lineage, containment, and sovereignty) using only offline, open-source verification tools.
