# Context: Phase 17.1 — Constitutional Compression & Semantic Lineage

## Objective
Preserve long-term institutional comprehensibility and replayability as constitutional evolution accumulates across multiple governance generations. Transform accumulated governance history into compressed, semantically stable institutional lineage.

## Requirements
- **Constitutional Compression Engine**:
    - `packages/constitutional-compression/`: Compress epochs, summarize mutation history.
- **Semantic Lineage Engine**:
    - `packages/semantic-lineage/`: Track invariant continuity, map semantics across generations.
- **Evolutionary Replay Compression**:
    - `packages/evolutionary-replay/`: Replay compressed epochs, validate summarized history.
- **Institutional Archaeology Layer**:
    - `packages/governance-archaeology/`: Reconstruct governance eras, explain mutation rationale.
- **CLI (ztanctl)**:
    - `governance lineage/compress/archaeology/evolution-map`.

## Strategic Intent
Avoid "constitutional complexity explosion" by prioritizing evolutionary legibility. Ensure that ZTAN remains understandable and replayable even after decades of controlled mutation.

## Success Criteria
- Constitutional evolution remains semantically stable across multiple policy epochs.
- Replay complexity remains bounded through epoch compression.
- Governance archaeology successfully reconstructs historical mutation rationales.
- Institutional memory survives deep evolutionary paths with forensic expansion integrity.
