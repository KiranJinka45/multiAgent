# Context: Phase 23.1 — External Institutional Pilot Exposure

## Objective
Replace simulated legitimacy with empirical legitimacy by exposing ZTAN to real external operators, real-world governance friction, and adversarial operational conditions. Transition from "Architecture-Derived Trust" to "History-Proven Trust."

## Requirements
- **External Operator Onboarding Layer**:
    - `packages/external-operator-onboarding/`: Automate and monitor the onboarding of independent external operators with varying expertise.
- **Adversarial Operational Stressor**:
    - `packages/adversarial-operational-stressor/`: Inject real-world friction (delayed recoveries, malformed attestations, cognitive bottlenecks) into pilots.
- **Governance Friction Monitor**:
    - `packages/governance-friction-monitor/`: Track and analyze misunderstandings, disputes, and operator errors during real usage.
- **Economic Stress Tester**:
    - `packages/economic-stress-tester/`: Validate resource constraints and retention costs under real operational budget pressure.
- **CLI (ztanctl exposure)**:
    - New suite of commands: `exposure onboard`, `exposure stress`, `exposure audit-friction`.

## Strategic Intent
Pivot from "conceptual saturation" to **Institutional Empiricism**. ZTAN's moat is now built through real operational exposure. This phase exposes the system's human-factor vulnerabilities, economic inefficiencies, and institutional friction, allowing it to accumulate historically undeniable legitimacy.

## Success Criteria
- Independent external operators successfully manage institutional cells under friction.
- Real-world governance friction is identified, analyzed, and mitigated.
- Economic survivability is proven under real operational budget constraints.
- ZTAN accumulates empirical uptime and recovery data from real usage.
