# Context: Phase 13.1R — Institutional Staking Hardening & Economic Governance Validation

## Objective
Transform existing staking infrastructure into institutionally survivable economic governance. Focus on economic survivability, governance legitimacy, anti-capture resistance, and institutional auditability.

## Requirements
- **Economic Governance Hardening**:
    - Implement concentration-risk analysis and capture detection in `staking-core`.
    - Implement slashing blast-radius estimation.
- **Survivability Engine**:
    - New `packages/staking-core/src/survivability/`: Governance shock modeling and stress testing.
- **Legitimacy Scoring**:
    - New `packages/staking-core/src/legitimacy/`: Decentralization and survivability scoring.
- **CLI (ztanctl)**:
    - `stake inspect/audit/survivability`
    - `governance-risk`
- **Documentation**:
    - `docs/STAKED_GOVERNANCE_SPEC.md`
    - `docs/GOVERNANCE_ECONOMICS_AUDIT.md`

## Strategic Intent
Prove that ZTAN economic incentives remain constitutionally bounded and that the network can survive adversarial economic pressure without systemic collapse.

## Recommended Approach
Implement soft concentration limits + governance slowdown mechanisms to resist capture while maintaining institutional flexibility.
