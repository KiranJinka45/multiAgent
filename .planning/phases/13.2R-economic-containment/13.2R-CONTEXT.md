# Context: Phase 13.2R — Economic Containment & Governance Stabilization

## Objective
Implement active containment mechanisms to prevent economic instability (slashing cascades, concentration events) from escalating into systemic governance collapse.

## Requirements
- **Containment Engine**:
    - `packages/staking-core/src/containment/`: Implement governance slowdown and slashing-rate throttling.
- **Circuit Breakers**:
    - `packages/staking-core/src/circuit-breakers/`: Pause high-risk transitions and unstable slashing cascades.
- **Stabilization Policies**:
    - `configs/governance-stabilization/`: Define thresholds for concentration, shock, and surges.
- **Recovery Logic**:
    - `packages/staking-core/src/recovery/`: Deterministic reconstruction and verification of containment actions.
- **CLI (ztanctl)**:
    - `containment status`
    - `governance stabilize`
    - `governance recover`
    - `circuit-breaker inspect`

## Strategic Intent
Pivot from passive observability to active institutional stabilization. Ensure that containment actions remain replayable, cryptographically provable, and constitutionally bounded.

## Recommended Approach
Hybrid containment: Automatic temporary containment at critical thresholds, requiring mandatory federated review for persistence.
