# Context: Phase 15.2 — Federated Institutional Trust Exchange

## Objective
Enable interoperable institutional trust continuity across multiple federations, organizations, and sovereign deployments.

## Requirements
- **Federated Trust Exchange Engine**:
    - `packages/federated-trust/`: Exchange and validate continuity attestations across organizations.
- **Institutional Reputation Layer**:
    - `packages/reputation-engine/`: Calculate reputation based on operational continuity, drift history, and replay reliability.
- **Cross-Federation Verification**:
    - `packages/federated-verification/`: Independently replay and verify foreign governance histories without privileged access.
- **CLI (ztanctl)**:
    - `federation trust/verify/continuity/reputation`.

## Strategic Intent
Transform "isolated continuity" into "federated reputation." Prove that institutions can verify each other's governance history without creator mediation, creating a compounded moat of interoperable trust.

## Success Criteria
- Institution A independently verifies Institution B's continuity history.
- Trust reputations are derive-able purely from operational evidence.
- Cross-federation replay is 100% deterministic.
- Sovereign governance boundaries are preserved during trust exchange.
