# Context: Phase 21.1 — Real-World Deployment Hardening & Operator Compression

## Objective
Transform ZTAN from a conceptually complete institutional architecture into a low-friction, operationally durable production substrate. Focus on deployment simplicity, operator cognition compression, and longitudinal evidence accumulation.

## Requirements
- **Operator Compression Engine**:
    - `packages/operator-compression/`: Summarize governance state and simplify incident recovery guidance to reduce cognitive burden.
- **Deployment Hardening Layer**:
    - `packages/deployment-hardening/`: Auto-detect risks and automate safety defaults to ensure zero-friction, safe institutional deployment.
- **Evidence Lifecycle Engine**:
    - `packages/evidence-lifecycle/`: Manage the archiving and compaction of replay evidence to ensure economic sustainability.
- **Institutional Dashboard Layer**:
    - `packages/institutional-dashboard/`: Provide high-level visualization of institutional health and drift summaries.
- **CLI (ztanctl ops)**:
    - New suite of commands for operational summary, diagnosis, compression, and archival.

## Strategic Intent
Pivot from architectural invention to **Operational Legitimacy Accumulation**. The value of ZTAN now comes from its ability to be deployed effortlessly, operated with minimal cognitive load, and sustained economically over decades. This phase hardens the operational reality of the platform.

## Success Criteria
- Deployment becomes a "boring," predictable institutional process.
- Operator cognitive load during incidents is significantly reduced through state compression.
- Evidence lifecycle policies ensure replayability remains economically viable.
- Operational telemetry becomes the primary driver of institutional trust.
