# Phase 08.2 Context: Institutional Evidence Packets

## Operational Intent
Phase 8.2 transitions the platform from "governed execution" to "verifiable operational truth." The goal is to produce **Replayable Evidence Packets** for every mission that satisfy institutional audit and risk-management requirements.

## Core Invariants: Evidence Packets

### 1. Merkle Lineage (Provenance)
- **Rule:** Every `FinalizedEnvelope` must include a Merkle Proof of inclusion in the mission's audit chain.
- **Verification:** Independent of the ZTAN runtime, a stakeholder should be able to verify that the mission's state transitions were monotonic and authorized.

### 2. Containment Proof (Blast Radius)
- **Rule:** Post-execution verification results (LOC delta, file globs, path traversal check) must be signed and included in the packet.
- **Verification:** Proves the mission stayed within its pre-declared mathematical boundaries.

### 3. Recovery Assurance (Rollback Proof)
- **Rule:** The `rollbackProof` generated by the `ShadowRollbackEngine` must be a mandatory field in the T3/T4 evidence packet.
- **Verification:** Proves the system was physically capable of reverting the mutation before the mutation was ever applied.

### 4. Sandbox Attestation
- **Rule:** Include isolation signatures from the `ISandboxProvider`.
- **Verification:** Proves the execution occurred inside the pre-declared isolation level (e.g., gVisor kernel, Firecracker VM).

### 5. Economic Rationality
- **Rule:** Final token consumption vs. cost-ceiling snapshot.
- **Verification:** Proves the mission operated within its economic budget.

## Longitudinal Trust: Mission Reputation
Initialize the following tracking signals to build operational trust over time:
- **Rollback Reliability:** % of successful pre-mutation drills.
- **Replay Stability:** % of missions with zero state drift on replay.
- **Policy Violation Rate:** Frequency of blast-radius breaches (safety pressure).
- **Cognitive Health:** Frequency of circuit-breaker/HITL trips (autonomy stability).

## Governance Rule: Reputation as Constraint
In later phases, a high "Mission Reputation" score will be a prerequisite for escalating missions from T2 to T3 without manual quorum overrides.
