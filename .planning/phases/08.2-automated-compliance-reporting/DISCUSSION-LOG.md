# Discussion Log: Phase 08.2 Institutional Evidence

**Date:** 2026-05-10
**Participants:** USER, Antigravity (the agent)

## Context
Phase 8.2 marks the transition from "hardened enforcement" to "verifiable truth." The discussion focused on how to transform raw execution logs into "Institutional Evidence Packets" that generate measurable trust.

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Evidence vs. Reporting
- **Decision:** Prioritize "Replayable Evidence Packets" over dashboards.
- **Rationale:** Dashboards are "compliance theater." Replayable packets (Merkle lineage, signed manifests, rollback proofs) are "forensic truth" that survive independent audit.

### 2. The Rollback Proof
- **Decision:** Mandatory inclusion of the pre-mutation Shadow Rollback signature in the evidence packet for T3+ missions.
- **Rationale:** Proving rollback capability *before* mutation is the ultimate operational credibility signal.

### 3. Mission Reputation
- **Decision:** Begin tracking longitudinal signals (Rollback reliability, Replay stability, Breach attempts).
- **Rationale:** Trust is not a boolean; it is an accumulated history of operational reliability.

### 4. Sandbox Attestation
- **Decision:** Include isolation level and runtime signatures in the packet.
- **Rationale:** Proves the "physical" containment level used for the mission.

### 5. Policy Enforcement as Asset
- **Decision:** The post-execution "Blast Radius Enforcement Proof" is a primary asset of the evidence packet.
- **Rationale:** Most systems only check intent; ZTAN checks the *actual* resulting mutation surface.

## Conclusion
The strategic objective for Phase 8.2 is to make the system's safety and governance **measurable and falsifiable** through machine-readable forensic evidence.
