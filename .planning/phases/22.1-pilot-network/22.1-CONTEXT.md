# Context: Phase 22.1 — Real Institutional Pilot Network & Longitudinal Telemetry

## Objective
Transform ZTAN from a mature operational substrate into a historically trusted operational ecosystem. Orchestrate a network of independent institutional pilots and accumulate longitudinal operational data as a core institutional asset.

## Requirements
- **Pilot Network Engine**:
    - `packages/pilot-network/`: Infrastructure to manage and monitor a distributed network of independent institutional pilot cells.
- **Longitudinal Telemetry Indexer**:
    - `packages/longitudinal-telemetry/`: Module to aggregate and index decadal operational history, identifying long-term stability patterns.
- **Trust History Asset Engine**:
    - `packages/trust-history-assets/`: Tooling to convert longitudinal evidence into verifiable institutional "Trust Certificates."
- **Operator Workflow Audit**:
    - `packages/operator-workflow-audit/`: Analysis of human operator behavior during pilots to refine workflows and cognitive compression.
- **CLI (ztanctl ecosystem)**:
    - New suite of commands: `ecosystem join`, `ecosystem stats`, `ecosystem history`.

## Strategic Intent
Pivot from architectural maturity to **Accumulated Operational Civilization History**. The ultimate moat for ZTAN is its record of uptime, replay continuity, and incident survivability under real-world institutional load. This phase begins the formal accumulation of that history at an ecosystem scale.

## Success Criteria
- Independent institutional pilots successfully join and operate within the ZTAN ecosystem.
- Longitudinal telemetry provides measurable proof of institutional stability over time.
- Trust history assets are successfully generated and verified between organizations.
- Operator workflows are refined based on real-world behavioral analysis.
