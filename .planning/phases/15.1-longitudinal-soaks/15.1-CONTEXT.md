# Context: Phase 15.1 — Longitudinal Deployment Soaks

## Objective
Prove governance continuity and institutional trust accumulation over extended operational windows. Shift focus from "invention" to "historical continuity."

## Strategic Intent
The platform's value now compounds through "boring operation." We must prove that trust persists under operator turnover, institutional drift, and long-term state accumulation.

## Requirements
- **Longitudinal Trust Monitor**:
    - Track "Trust Days" (continuous stable operation).
    - Monitor governance interpretation consistency over time.
- **Institutional Drift Detector**:
    - Identify deviations from the constitutional anchor or institutional profile.
- **Continuity Engine**:
    - Ensure replay durability and evidence completeness over long-term lineages.
- **CLI (ztanctl)**:
    - `soak status`: View longitudinal health.
    - `soak drift`: Check for institutional deviation.
    - `soak continuity`: Validate evidence durability.

## Success Criteria
- 0% drift from constitutional anchor over simulated long-term windows.
- 100% replay integrity for events > 30 days old (simulated).
- High operator interpretation consistency across simulated turnover.
