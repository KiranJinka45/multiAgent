# Context: Phase 18.1 — Federated Semantic Alignment & Invariant Consensus

## Objective
Ensure that independently evolving federations can maintain compatible institutional semantics and preserve shared invariant interpretations across centuries of independent evolution. Transform "replayable institutional meaning" into "shared civilizational semantic continuity."

## Requirements
- **Semantic Alignment Engine**:
    - `packages/semantic-alignment/`: Compare semantic maps between federations and detect conceptual drift.
- **Invariant Consensus Protocol**:
    - `packages/invariant-consensus/`: Negotiate and verify shared interpretations of core invariants (Determinism, Sovereignty, etc.).
- **Semantic Treaty Layer**:
    - `packages/semantic-treaties/`: Formalize inter-org semantic agreements and translations.
- **CLI (ztanctl)**:
    - `federation align/treaty/invariant-check`.

## Strategic Intent
Prevent "civilizational governance fragmentation" where federations remain technically compatible but become semantically incompatible. Ensure that the "Spirit" of ZTAN remains unified even as its "Letter" diverges across independent institutions.

## Success Criteria
- Independent federations can reach consensus on the interpretation of core constitutional invariants.
- Semantic drift between organizations is detected and measurable.
- Inter-org semantic treaties successfully reconcile diverging governance terminologies.
- Civilizational semantic continuity survives deep, independent institutional evolution.
