# Context: Phase 20.1 — Production-Pilot Consolidation & Adversarial Stress Testing

## Objective
Pivot from architectural invention to longitudinal operational evidence by deploying production-grade execution cells and validating ZTAN's resilience against real-world adversarial telemetry and economic load.

## Requirements
- **Production-Pilot Engine**:
    - `packages/production-pilot/`: Infrastructure for deploying and managing high-assurance institutional execution cells.
- **Adversarial Telemetry Engine**:
    - `packages/adversarial-telemetry/`: Collection and analysis of operational data from real-world (or high-fidelity simulated) adversarial stress tests.
- **Economic Sustainability Audit**:
    - `packages/economic-audit/`: Formal verification of the institution's fiscal rationality and long-term economic survivability.
- **CLI (ztanctl)**:
    - `pilot deploy/status`, `audit economic-sustainability`.

## Strategic Intent
Transition ZTAN into its final stage of maturity: **Operational Reality.** The architecture is conceptually complete; its value now comes from its ability to survive real-world institutional stress, adversarial attacks, and economic shifts over time. This phase consolidates the pilot infrastructure into a production-ready operational substrate.

## Success Criteria
- Production-pilot cells successfully survive high-load adversarial stress tests without loss of trust continuity.
- Adversarial telemetry identifies and mitigates real-world edge cases in governance logic.
- The Economic Sustainability Audit proves the institution's long-term fiscal rationality.
- ZTAN is operationally ready for wide-scale institutional adoption.
