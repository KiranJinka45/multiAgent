# Requirements: Milestone 35 — Maintenance Era & Real Production Stewardship

## Mission
The mission of this phase is NOT to expand the platform. It is to preserve operational reliability, maintainability, auditability, simplicity, and long-term survivability through disciplined, low-entropy stewardship over extended real-world operation.

## Governing Principle
The platform is considered successful when operators rarely think about it, incidents remain understandable, maintenance remains predictable, onboarding remains reproducible, recovery remains deterministic, and operational complexity stays low over time.

"Operational longevity without institutional entropy."

---

## Objective 1: Long-Term Production Stewardship
Operate the platform continuously under real production conditions.

### Requirements
- Maintain stable operator rotation.
- Preserve reproducible onboarding.
- Maintain deterministic recovery.
- Enforce governance restraint.
- Minimize operational cognitive load.

### Success Metrics
- Uptime history (target 99.99%+).
- Maintenance frequency (stable trend).
- Operator workload and alert fatigue monitoring.
- Infrastructure drift detection.

---

## Objective 2: Real Incident & Failure Governance
Treat failures as permanent operational learning records.

### Requirements
- Create reproducible RCA for every incident.
- Archive evidence and identify procedural weaknesses.
- Identify observability gaps and human coordination failures.
- Classify entropy sources.

---

## Objective 3: Dependency & Infrastructure Sustainability
Continuously evaluate long-term infrastructure health.

### Requirements
- Evaluate dependency aging and certificate expiration risk.
- Validate cloud portability and storage sustainability.
- Perform backup restoration drills and disaster portability exercises.
- Validate certificate rotation without downtime.

---

## Objective 4: Operational Simplicity Preservation
Prevent silent operational complexity growth.

### Requirements
- Measure dashboard growth and telemetry noise.
- Identify and remove undocumented procedures or workarounds.
- Remove non-actionable alerts and unused abstractions.
- Monitor cognitive load trends for operators.

---

## Objective 5: External Reproducibility & Auditability
Maintain external credibility through reproducibility.

### Requirements
- Ensure external audit reproducibility.
- Independent recovery validation (operator-independent).
- Legally defensible lineage integrity.
- Public audit reconstruction and evidence replay.

---

## Final Required Output
A continuously maintained operational dossier titled:
**"Nexus ZTAN — Production Stewardship & Maintenance Record"**

Including:
1. Long-term uptime history
2. Maintenance activity history
3. Incident & recovery archive
4. Dependency lifecycle status
5. Backup & restoration validation
6. External audit reproducibility findings
7. Entropy & complexity trends
8. Operational burden metrics
9. Governance consistency observations
10. Infrastructure sustainability analysis
11. Human independence validation updates
12. Remaining operational risks
13. Evidence-backed stewardship conclusions
