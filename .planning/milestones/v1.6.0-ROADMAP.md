# Stewardship Campaign Epochs

Rather than launching new abstract engineering phases, ZTAN's validation and operational posture is organized into continuous, data-driven **Stewardship Campaign Epochs** targeting real infrastructure and team behaviors:

- **Epoch A: 30-Day Thaw Normalization Tracking**
  - *Goal:* Monitor thaw bypass events. Track SRE override concentration (HHI index) and slope values to ensure thaws are rare exceptions rather than normalized habits.
- **Epoch B: Succession Survivability Drills**
  - *Goal:* Periodically run simulated organizational handoffs using the `SuccessionSimulator`. Verify that fresh SRE operators can successfully diagnose and restore systems using accrued playbooks and override traceability.
- **Epoch C: Replay Archaeology Degradation Benchmarking**
  - *Goal:* Audit compressed causal narrative fidelity. Verify that semantic equivalence remains high even when historical telemetry is dramatically compressed.
- **Epoch D: Simplification Effectiveness Measurement**
  - *Goal:* Quantify cyclomatic complexity, LOC reduction, and import graph compaction across destructive simplification campaigns.
- **Epoch E: Telemetry Reduction Survivability Tests**
  - *Goal:* Conduct blind recovery drills where >50% of telemetry metrics are intentionally retired. Certify if operators can still isolate root causes with sparse evidence.
- **Epoch F: Operator Cognition Fatigue Monitoring**
  - *Goal:* Collect operator response MTTR, false-positive alert fatigue, and cognitive load levels during simulated compound failure storms.
- **Epoch G: Resurrection Prediction Accuracy Benchmarking**
  - *Goal:* Evaluate operator forecasting accuracy. Flag optimism bias and collective optimism synchronization where teams reinforce incorrect assumptions.

### Concrete Next Maintenance Workstreams

1. **Continuous Certification CI Pipeline**:
   - Integrate the `ReplayCertificationHarness` directly into the CI validation stages.
   - Run replay regression verification on the `.ztan/corpus/` immutable quarantine snapshots upon every commit in version-pinned sandboxes to prevent regression.
2. **Stewardship & SLO Reporting**:
   - Automate monthly operational reports tracking actual WAL bytes growth rates, average MTTR during SRE drills, and CPU loop lag trends, segmented into Target, Observed, and Certified envelopes.
3. **Complexity & Deletion Quotas**:
   - Enforce CI complexity budgets (AST cyclomatic complexity <= 10, nesting depth <= 3, function length <= 50 lines) and deletion quotas (force removal of `@temporary-exception` markers within 2 commits) to prevent exception-creep.
   - Implement deterministic AST architectural diff scoring, import graph expansion budgets, and subsystem coupling thresholds to block convenient operator abstractions or dependency fan-out growth.
   - Enforce multidimensional governance budgets as placeholder constants, calibrating them longitudinally into empirically tuned envelopes (CI runtime <= 5m, active policy rule count <= 15, SRE override coordination <= 15m, alert page volume < 1/day).
   - Enforce a strict, exceptionless governance recursion ceiling (max 2 tiers: `Core Invariant -> Validator -> Audit`) with no emergency override or temporary review bypasses permitted, ensuring meta-governance limit enforcement is purely mechanical.
   - Implement the Compressive Governance Principle (capping governance LOC < 20% of runtime size) and perform quarterly Governance Deduplication Auditing and Metric Conflict Analysis to prune competing metrics and redundant rules.
   - Enforce strict Evidence Surface Budgets for all new telemetry metrics (requiring explicit definition of operational purpose, footprint cost, retention curve, SRE actionability, and deletion criteria).
   - Enforce the Telemetry Schema Version Migration Policy (blocking modifications to evidence schemas without forward/backward compatibility mapping to protect historical replay archaeology).

4. **Survivability & Compound Campaigns**:
   - Conduct periodic blind recovery exercises and verify cold-start onboarding.
   - Execute compound pathology campaigns (scheduler starvation + WAL stalls, replica lag + connection storms, partition asymmetry + delayed checkpoint flushes, clock skew + replay determinism).
   - Run upgrade reversibility drills, degraded-environment recovery testing, and WAL corruption archaeology.
   - Model and validate SRE playbooks against economic distortion and commercial pressure pathways (e.g. operator deadline pressure, staff/budget reductions, alert silencing, and gate bypass incentives).
5. **Longitudinal Evidence Campaigns (The Saturation Pivot)**:
   - Declare governance theory and policy rule refinement halted at the current saturation threshold. All subsequent waves focus entirely on empirical validation, long-horizon soak campaigns, and observational evidence collection to back certified envelopes.
   - Track 3-6 month drift curves, runtime evolution regressions, WAL amplification trends, and observability ROI trends in `.ztan/evidence-vault/`.
   - Profile MTTR evolution and operator ambiguity mapping over elapsed time.
6. **Realism Hardening & Compaction Priorities**:
   - **Multi-Day Reality Soaks**: Move from brief verification runs to 24h, 72h, and 1-week continuous soak campaigns, measuring heap slopes, replay graph sizes, false positive rates, and operator telemetry burdens.
   - **Real Infrastructure Validation**: Transition from application-layer proxies and monkey-patching to actual Linux `tc/netem` packet delays, cgroup-based hardware limit enforcement, physical PostgreSQL standby node replication lag drills, and power-loss barrier disk write corruptions.
   - **Evidence Lifecycle Compaction**: Mitigate telemetry explosion and graph database scalability limits by implementing evidence compaction, causality-based pruning, database partition stratification, and aging heuristics.
   - **Operator Ergonomics & Advisory-Only Limits**: Limit Phase 19 (Policy Retirement) strictly to SRE-advisory reports; prohibit automated deletion of active constraints to avoid survivorship bias traps. Build incident-level timeline collapses and cognitive summary views to support teams under extreme pressure.
7. **Negative Growth Enforcement & Telemetry Consolidation**:
   - Enforce a strict "telemetry deletion quota" requiring that any new metric or log field must be offset by removing or consolidating existing telemetry.
   - Ban high-cardinality metadata schemas to prevent hidden feature creep under the guise of "evidence collection."
8. **Evidence Vault Compaction & Tiering Mechanics**:
   - Establish explicit retention tiering and forensic sampling rules.
   - Implement causal graph pruning, causality collapse heuristics, and cold archive segments to prevent graph database scalability limits and telemetry overhead instability.
9. **Empirical Operator Cognition & Simplification**:
   - Audit operator response confusion, dashboard overload, replay reconstruction latency, false-positive fatigue, escalation ambiguity, and time-to-root-cause.
   - Restrict organizational drift telemetry strictly to weak indicators of SRE routing/onboarding decay; avoid predictive sociology or attempting to deeply quantify complex political and social adaptations.
