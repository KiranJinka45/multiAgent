# Governance Explainability Guide (V1.0)

## 1. Interpretive Philosophy
Every action taken by the ZTAN governance system must be explainable in plain institutional language. We avoid technical jargon ("Nakamoto Coefficient", "Merkle Proof") in favor of institutional concepts ("Decentralization Health", "Evidence Chain").

## 2. Common Explanations

### 2.1 Why was my stake slashed?
**Explanation**: Your federation double-signed a governance root in Epoch X. This is a violation of the "Anti-Equivocation" rule. 100% of your collateral was burned to preserve the integrity of the network.

### 2.2 Why did the circuit breaker trip?
**Explanation**: More than 25% of the network's total trust weight was lost in a single epoch. This indicates a potential systemic failure. High-risk transitions have been paused to prevent a governance fork.

## 3. Constitutional Justification
All containment actions are justified under the **Sovereign Stability** mandate. This mandate authorizes the system to temporarily restrict liquidity or governance speed to ensure that the network survives adversarial pressure.
