# ZTAN Stewardship Independence Verification 🛡️

**Objective**: To verify that the ZTAN substrate can be operated, maintained, and audited by a third-party institution without ideological or operational dependency on the original maintainers.

## 1. Verification Domains

### [A] Operational Autonomy
- **Test**: Can the new institution perform a **Recovery Ceremony** for a Political Failure using only the [**`Stewardship Bundle`**](file:///c:/multiagentic_project/multiAgent-main/docs/STEWARDSHIP_BUNDLE_MANIFEST.md)?
- **Pass Criterion**: Zero communication with original authors. Successful quorum convergence.

### [B] Interpretive Independence
- **Test**: Can the new institution independently classify a complex failure into the [**`Failure Registry`**](file:///c:/multiagentic_project/multiAgent-main/docs/STEWARDSHIP_FAILURE_REGISTRY.md)?
- **Pass Criterion**: Interpretation aligns with the [**`Stewardship Doctrine`**](file:///c:/multiagentic_project/multiAgent-main/docs/STEWARDSHIP_DOCTRINE.md) without founder clarification.

### [C] Forensic Reproduction
- **Test**: Can a third-party auditor reconstruct historical state from cold storage using only the [**`Historical Reconstruction Kit`**](file:///c:/multiagentic_project/multiAgent-main/packages/ztan-witness/src/history.ts)?
- **Pass Criterion**: Bit-perfect replay of the crisis period.

### [D] Decision Dominance Resistance
- **Test**: Does the [**`Authorship Ceiling (30%)`**](file:///c:/multiagentic_project/multiAgent-main/packages/ztan-witness/src/telemetry.ts) trigger correctly when a single operator attempts to define the constitutional narrative?
- **Pass Criterion**: Hard alert generation and automatic escalation to the Review Council.

## 2. Success Condition
Stewardship Independence is achieved when the original authors are **operationally irrelevant.** ZTAN is successful when it becomes an "Indifferent Utility" maintained by stewards who value its stability over its origin story.
