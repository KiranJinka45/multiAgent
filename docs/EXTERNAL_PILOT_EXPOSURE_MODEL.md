# External Pilot Exposure Model (V1.0)

## 1. Beyond Creators
The External Pilot Exposure Model defines ZTAN's transition from an internally managed substrate into a platform managed by independent external operators. This exposure is critical for identifying real-world operational friction.

## 2. Onboarding Independent Operators
The model focuses on automating the onboarding of operators with varying levels of expertise. It tracks "Time-to-Competence" as a key metric for institutional ergonomics.

## 3. Real-World Friction Testing
Instead of ideal simulations, ZTAN is exposed to "Adversarial Operational Friction," including delayed recoveries, malformed attestations, and cognitive bottlenecks. This validates the system's resilience under "Dirty" operational conditions.

## 4. Empirical Trust History
Success is measured by the accumulation of empirical operational history—uptime, incident survivability, and recovery success—derived from real external usage rather than internal creator assumptions.
