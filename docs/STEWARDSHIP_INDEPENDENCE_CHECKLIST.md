# ZTAN Stewardship Independence Checklist 🛡️

**Objective**: To validate the substrate's survival without founder arbitration or original author assistance.

## 1. Operational Independence
- [ ] **Founder Absence Test**: Can a restoration ceremony be completed without the original authors participating?
- [ ] **Independent Incident Handling**: Can external stewards diagnose a replay divergence using ONLY the [**`STEWARDSHIP_FAILURE_REGISTRY.md`**](file:///c:/multiagentic_project/multiAgent-main/docs/STEWARDSHIP_FAILURE_REGISTRY.md)?
- [ ] **Board-Only Recovery**: Can a political restoration be authorized without "expert deference" to the original creators?

## 2. Interpretive Independence
- [ ] **Doctrine Interpretation**: Can a new board interpret [**`STEWARDSHIP_DOCTRINE.md`**](file:///c:/multiagentic_project/multiAgent-main/docs/STEWARDSHIP_DOCTRINE.md) correctly during a high-stakes dispute?
- [ ] **Simplification Decisions**: Can external stewards identify and remove a rule without seeking "founder approval"?
- [ ] **Dissent Production**: Are second-generation stewards authoring [**`MinorityAnnotations`**](file:///c:/multiagentic_project/multiAgent-main/packages/ztan-witness/src/history.ts) that are evidentiary and independent?

## 3. Forensic Independence
- [ ] **External Replay Verification**: Can an independent institution reproduce the entire lineage from cold storage without maintainer assistance?
- [ ] **Evidence Recovery**: Can the [**`Production Manifest`**](file:///c:/multiagentic_project/multiAgent-main/docs/ZTAN_PRODUCTION_MANIFEST.md) be used to reconstruct state on new, non-maintainer hardware?

## 4. Succession Readiness
- [ ] **Rotation Handover**: Is the 180-day rotation happening without "knowledge siloing"?
- [ ] **Anti-Priesthood Education**: Have new stewards completed the historical reconstruction training?
