# Operational Maturity Model (V1.0)

## 1. From Invention to Reality
The Operational Maturity Model defines ZTAN's transition from an architectural project into a stable, production-ready institutional execution environment. Maturity is measured through longitudinal evidence and real-world stress resilience.

## 2. Production-Pilot Cells
High-assurance execution cells are the building blocks of the ZTAN network. These cells are deployed with production-grade monitoring, resource isolation, and institutional governance roots.

## 3. Longitudinal Trust Evidence
Unlike "social trust," ZTAN's trust is derived from the accumulation of operational telemetry over long timelines. A "Mature" institution is one that has survived years of adversarial load while maintaining 100% replay integrity.

## 4. Institutional Stability Metrics
- **Survivability Index**: The ability to maintain operations during extreme adversarial stress.
- **Fiscal Rationality**: The long-term economic sustainability of the institutional cell.
- **Replay Continuity**: 100% verifiable history across all operational cycles.
