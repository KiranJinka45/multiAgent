# ZTAN Constitutional Normalization Policy

**Objective**: To ensure that emergency degraded continuity and probationary suspensions are temporary and that full constitutional legitimacy is restored.

## 1. Legitimacy Healing (The Recovery Loop)
- **Retrospective Validation**: Any epoch flagged as `DEGRADED_LEGITIMACY` MUST undergo a retrospective healing vote once the crisis has passed.
- **Healing Quorum**: Restoration of full legitimacy requires the standard super-quorum (67%+) anchored in a reconciled forensic audit.
- **Evidentiary Standard**: Healing is only permitted if independent auditors can verify the integrity of the execution lineage during the degraded period.

## 2. Probationary Expiry
- **Automatic Expiry**: All probationary suspensions of governance rules have a hard-coded 30-day expiry.
- **Renewal Restriction**: A suspension cannot be renewed more than once without a full 75% Constitutional Referendum.
- **Mandatory Post-Mortem**: Every suspension MUST trigger an automated Post-Mortem report evaluating the "Operational Friction" that necessitated the retreat.

## 3. Review Council Oversight
- **Deadlock Resolution**: Rulings by the `ReviewCouncil` are intended to break deadlocks, not create permanent exceptions.
- **Precedent Transparency**: All Council Opinions are archived and publicly auditable to prevent the emergence of "Shadow Governance."
- **Normalcy Target**: The Stewardship Committee MUST prioritize returning the system to "Standard Normalcy" within 14 days of any emergency intervention.

## 4. Normalization Monitoring
- **Exception Dependency Rate**: If the `EXCEPTION_RATE` exceeds 10% over any 30-day period, the Stewardship Committee MUST initiate a root-cause analysis of constitutional overfitting.
- **Healing Success Rate**: Tracking the frequency of successful retrospective legitimacy restorations.
