# Operational Economics Policy (V1.0)

## 1. Budgeting for Transparency
Institutional transparency has real-world economic costs (storage, replay, verification). The Operational Economics Policy defines how institutions manage their fiscal resources under real budget pressure.

## 2. Retention vs. Cost
The policy establishes standards for balancing evidence retention requirements with storage costs. It mandates the use of proof compaction and tiered archival to ensure economic survivability.

## 3. Economic Stress Testing
The `EconomicStressTester` validates that institutional cells can maintain economic rationality and full replay integrity even when operating under severe budget constraints.

## 4. Sustainable Institutional Growth
As the ecosystem scales, economic efficiency becomes a primary differentiator for institutional trust. Solvency and fiscal rationality are treated as core primitives of institutional reputation.
