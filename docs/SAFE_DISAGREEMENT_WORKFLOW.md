# Safe Disagreement Workflow (V1.0)

## 1. Managing Policy Conflict
When two federations disagree on the interpretation of a policy or the validity of a governance action, they trigger the Safe Disagreement Workflow.

## 2. Formal Dispute Recording
Disagreements are formally recorded and cryptographically anchored. This ensures that the dispute is transparent and historically auditable, preventing "Silent Schisms."

## 3. Trust Continuity during Disputes
Crucially, ZTAN maintains **Trust Continuity** while a dispute is active. Federations can continue to interact technically (settlements, attestations) even if they are in a formal constitutional disagreement, provided their core invariants remain aligned.

## 4. Conflict Reconciliation
Disagreements are eventually reconciled through semantic treaties or global invariant consensus. The goal is not to force immediate agreement, but to allow for safe, managed divergence.
