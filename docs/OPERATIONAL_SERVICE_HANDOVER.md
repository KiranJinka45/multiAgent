# ZTAN Operational Service Handover (v1.0.0-PROD) 🛡️

**Objective**: To transition ZTAN from a "Development Project" to a "Stable Operational Service."

## 1. The Service Mandate
ZTAN is now a **Stable Institutional Service.**
- **No Roadmap**: There are no planned feature updates or architectural expansions.
- **No Velocity**: Success is measured by **Stability** and **Restraint**, not code volume or change frequency.
- **Service Level Objectives (SLOs)**:
    - **Replay Determinism**: 100% Correctness.
    - **Auditability**: 100% Forensic Traceability.
    - **Legitimacy Health**: >90% Constitutional Sincerity.

- **Rule**: **Automate Mechanics, Not Accountability.**
- **Action**: All constitutional justifications MUST be manually authored and evidentiary. Rubber-stamp automation is a failure state.
- **Biennial Decommissioning Drill**: Mandatory simulation of the [**`DECOMMISSIONING_PROCEDURE.md`**](file:///c:/multiagentic_project/multiAgent-main/docs/DECOMMISSIONING_PROCEDURE.md) (Archival Seal, Keys Rotation, Final Replay).

- **Freeze Integrity Audits**: Every 180 days, ensuring replay/build reproducibility under hardware drift.

All project communication must use **Operational Language**:
- **Incident**: A technical or constitutional divergence.
- **Audit**: A forensic review of evidence.
- **Simplification**: The removal of procedural complexity.
- **Stewardship**: The maintenance of legitimacy.
