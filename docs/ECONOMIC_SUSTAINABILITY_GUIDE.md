# Economic Sustainability Guide (V1.0)

## 1. Fiscal Rationality in Institutions
Long-lived institutions must be economically sustainable. The Economic Sustainability Guide defines how ZTAN execution cells manage their fiscal resources and maintain long-term solvency.

## 2. Resource Metering and Settlement
All institutional actions in ZTAN are metered and settled using the platform's economic engines. This ensures that resource consumption is always backed by real-world economic value, preventing "Resource Exhaustion" attacks.

## 3. Sustainability Auditing
The `EconomicAudit` engine performs formal fiscal audits of institutional cells, predicting their long-term survivability based on current operational data and economic trends.

## 4. The Path to Solvency
For an institution to be considered "Mature," it must prove a projected survivability of decades under varying economic loads. This proof becomes a core primitive of institutional reputation.
