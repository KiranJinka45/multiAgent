# ZTAN Institutional Cognitive Cost Framework 🛡️

**Objective**: To monitor human attention exhaustion and operator friction as the primary threats to institutional durability.

## 1. Primary Metrics
| Metric | Signal of Pathology | Institutional Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Escalation Hesitation** | Operators delaying formal restoration ceremonies. | Simplify the Ceremony. |
| **Onboarding Fatigue** | New stewards failing to complete reconstruction drills. | Reduce the Doctrine. |
| **Doctrine Misinterpretation** | Recurring confusion over [**`Stewardship Vows`**](file:///c:/multiagentic_project/multiAgent-main/docs/STEWARDSHIP_DOCTRINE.md). | Plain Language Refactor. |
| **Audit Abandonment** | Low participation in [**`Randomized Forensic Sampling`**](file:///c:/multiagentic_project/multiAgent-main/packages/ztan-witness/src/telemetry.ts). | Reduce Audit Surface. |
| **Symbolic Overload** | Copy-pasting justification text in ceremonies. | Eliminate Symbolic Steps. |

## 2. The Indifference Threshold
A successful institutional utility must survive **Indifferent Adoption.**
- **Rule**: If adoption requires "Governance Enthusiasm," the substrate has failed its infrastructural mandate.
- **Rule**: The system must remain operable by stewards who do not "care" about the project's history or vision.

## 3. Negative Finding Mandate
If an external trial reveals that operators are bypassing process due to cognitive cost, this is **Positive Institutional Data.** The failure MUST be published in the [**`STEWARDSHIP_FAILURE_REGISTRY.md`**](file:///c:/multiagentic_project/multiAgent-main/docs/STEWARDSHIP_FAILURE_REGISTRY.md).

## 4. Maintenance Criterion
The primary success of ZTAN is the **preservation of human attention.** Any mechanism that consumes attention without providing decisive forensic evidence is a candidate for **Aggressive Subtraction.**
