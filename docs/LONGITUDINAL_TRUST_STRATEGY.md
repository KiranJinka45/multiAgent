# Longitudinal Trust Strategy (V1.0)

## 1. The Moat of Continuity
The primary competitive advantage of ZTAN institutional infrastructure is not feature velocity, but **Historical Continuity**. Trust in a governance system is earned through months and years of stable, boring, and predictable operation.

## 2. Trust Accumulation Primitives
- **Trust Days**: Every day of operation without unauthorized drift or containment failure increases the system's "Trust Weight."
- **Interpretive Stability**: The ability for new operators to reach consistent conclusions about historical events.
- **Forensic Permanence**: The guarantee that evidence packs generated months ago remain verifiable today.

## 3. Drift Management
To prevent institutional drift, the system periodically audits its own operational state against the **Locked Institutional Profile**. Any deviation in policy (e.g., unauthorized slashing changes) is flagged as a high-severity incident.

## 4. Operator Turnover Resilience
The system must survive the "generation gap" between different sets of operators. This is achieved through:
- **Narrative Continuity**: Explainability engines that generate human-readable history.
- **Constitutional Gating**: Preventing new operators from making "innovative" but destabilizing changes without dual-quorum approval.
