# Platform Finality Model

This document defines the requirements and procedures for ZTAN's transition from an evolving architecture to a stable institutional substrate.

## 1. Primitive Freezing
- **Core Immutability**: All constitutional primitives (Governance Roots, Merkle Engines, Replay Protocols) must be frozen.
- **Freeze Registry**: Centralized mapping of module stability (Experimental vs Frozen).
- **Contract Enforcement**: Frozen modules reject any modification that violates the established institutional contract.

## 2. Operational Entropy Reduction
- **Path Pruning**: Elimination of redundant governance workflows and duplicate operational paths.
- **Cognitive Load Optimization**: Designing interfaces and CLI commands to minimize the mental overhead for institutional operators.

## 3. Maintenance Survivability
- **Decadal Durability**: Ensuring the platform can be maintained over 10+ year horizons with minimal architectural drift.
- **Stability Certification**: Quantitative assessment of a node's readiness for long-term production finality.
