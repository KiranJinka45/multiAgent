# Governance Activation Workflow (V1.0)

## 1. Zero-Trust Initialization
The ZTAN governance engine is dormant until a successful **Readiness Validation** is performed. This ensures that no institution can operationalize a compromised or misconfigured environment.

## 2. Activation Sequence
1. **Evidence Sync**: Verify that the local governance lineage is anchored to the Global Trust Root.
2. **Containment Lock**: Active rate-throttling is initialized based on the selected Institutional Profile.
3. **Audit Readiness**: The Simulator verifies that the initial state is replayable by external parties.
4. **Final Certification**: The `Constitutional Anchor` signs off on the deployment, activating the governance mandate.

## 3. Operational Safe-Guards
Once activated, certain governance primitives are **Immutable** for the first 7 days. This "Stability Window" prevents high-frequency changes during the critical initial onboarding period.
