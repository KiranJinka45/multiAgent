## 1. The Discovery of "Sociological Decay"
The most profound discovery of the 90-day pilot was that **Legitimacy Failure begins long before Technical Failure.** The substrate remained bit-perfect while the human layer experienced rapid erosion.

## 2. Institutional Pathology Signals
We have identified the following patterns as precursors to institutional collapse:
- **Shadow Governance**: Operators coordinating informally to bypass official process.
- **Ritual Hollowing**: Copy-pasting justifications for restoration ceremonies.
- **Decision Dominance**: A single institution authoring the majority of the constitutional narrative.
- **Emergency Normalization**: Treating exception-handling as operational routine.

## 3. The Efficacy of Reduction
ZTAN improved its MTTR and Legitimacy Health ONLY when we **removed rules** (72h cool-down) and **constrained noise** (minority annotations). Complexity was identified as the primary threat to institutional durability.
