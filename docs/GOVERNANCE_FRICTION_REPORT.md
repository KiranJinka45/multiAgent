# Governance Friction Report (V1.0)

## 1. Tracking Institutional Weakness
The Governance Friction Report analyzes misunderstandings, disputes, and operator errors during real usage. It identifies systemic institutional weaknesses that theory and internal testing missed.

## 2. Operator Error Analysis
The report tracks the "Operator Confusion Rate" and ranks friction points based on frequency and impact. This data is used to further refine the platform's cognitive compression and UX.

## 3. Dispute Patterns
By monitoring how disagreements over policy or interpretation arise in the real world, ZTAN can refine its pluralistic interop bridges and constitutional alignment protocols.

## 4. Continuous Institutional Hardening
Governance friction is treated as a "Learning Asset," allowing the platform to harden its institutional primitives against the realities of human operational behavior.
