# Federated Recovery Workflow (V1.0)

## 1. The Path to Re-Certification
Quarantine is not necessarily permanent. A degraded federation can rebuild its trust reputation through a structured **Recovery Workflow**.

## 2. Recovery Steps
1. **Incident Resolution**: The organization must document and resolve the technical or governance failure that led to quarantine.
2. **Forensic Replay**: External auditors perform a full replay of the incident and resolution history.
3. **Lineage Re-Anchoring**: The organization synchronizes a new "Recovery Anchor" with the Global Trust Root.
4. **Gradual Restoration**: Reputation score is restored in stages (e.g., 25% per month of stable operation).

## 3. Recovery Evidence Packs
Organizations seeking recovery must provide **Recovery Evidence Packs**, which contain the full forensic history of the incident, the fix, and the new constitutional anchor.

## 4. Federated Peer Approval
While recovery is evidence-based, peers may perform independent verification of the recovery packs before resuming full trust exchange.
