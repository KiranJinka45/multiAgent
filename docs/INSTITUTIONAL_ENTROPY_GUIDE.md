# Institutional Entropy Guide (V1.0)

## 1. Detecting the Slow Decay
Institutional Entropy is the gradual accumulation of complacency, boredom, and shortcuts within an organization. This guide defines how to identify the early warning signs of decadal institutional decay.

## 2. The Over-Trust Index
The "Over-Trust Index" measures an organization's dangerous reliance on automated assumptions over manual verification. A rising index indicates a systemic decline in institutional vigilance.

## 3. Ritual Decay
"Audit Ritual Decay" occurs when governance ceremonies become symbolic routines. The guide outlines methods for re-injecting semantic meaning and rigor into these critical institutional workflows.

## 4. Resisting Monotony
To resist entropy, ZTAN institutions must use "Complacency Stressors"—unexpected verification prompts and monotony breaks—to maintain operator alertness during long periods of stability.
