# Nexus ZTAN: Operator Certification Standard (v2026.LTS.1)

## Overview
This document defines the requirements for a "Certified ZTAN Operator." Certification ensures that an individual can maintain, recover, and govern the Nexus ZTAN platform without reliance on tribal knowledge or creator intervention.

---

## 🎖️ Level 1: Operational Proficiency

An operator must demonstrate the ability to navigate the unified `ztanctl` interface and interpret system health.

### Requirements:
- **Baseline Health**: Ability to diagnose cluster quorum and link status using `ztanctl infra health`.
- **Identity Management**: Ability to register new operational identities and verify their hardware-bound attestations.
- **Friction Reporting**: Proactive identification of operational ambiguities via `ztanctl diag friction`.

---

## 🎖️ Level 2: Recovery Stewardship

The hallmark of a ZTAN Operator is the ability to restore state determinism under pressure.

### Requirements:
- **OCR Execution**: Successful execution of `ztanctl recovery execute` in a clean-room environment.
- **Determinism Verification**: Confirming that the restored system hash matches the institutional anchor.
- **Drill Performance**: Completion of the `corruption` and `rotation` drills within the 30-minute recovery window.

---

## 🎖️ Level 3: Governance Authority

Level 3 operators are authorized to participate in Multi-Party Approvals (MPA) and Trust Epoch rotations.

### Requirements:
- **Epoch Management**: Understanding the grace period mechanics and the `EpochTransitionManifest` lifecycle.
- **MPA Participation**: Demonstrated understanding of "Separation of Powers" by reviewing and signing `PatchIntent` and `GovernanceManifests`.
- **Trust Reporting**: Ability to generate and interpret the `governance report` and AIBOM artifacts.

---

## 🎓 Certification Drill (Clean-Room)

To achieve certification, a candidate must perform a **Zero-Knowledge Recovery**:
1. **Scenario**: A random "Chaos Event" is injected by the Proctor.
2. **Constraint**: No internet access to external documentation (only local playbooks).
3. **Success**: System restored and verified in < 30 minutes.
4. **Outcome**: Signed `CertificationProof` generated by `ztanctl governance report`.
