# ZTAN Stewardship Board Manifest (v1.0.0-PILOT) 🛡️

**Objective**: To enforce the Architectural Freeze and maintain constitutional continuity during the 90-day Pilot.

## 1. Board Composition
| Role | Responsibility | Authority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stewardship Lead** | Freeze Enforcement & Simplification | Unilateral Veto on Semantic Expansion |
| **External Auditor** | Independent Replay & Forensic Review | Non-repudiable Forensic Reports |
| **Operator Rep (x2)** | Operational Feedback & Runbook Health | Escalation Requests |
| **Minority Observer** | Dissent Preservation & Historiography | Mandatory Historiography Annotations |
| **Incident Coordinator** | Adversarial Drill & Crisis Orchestration | Chaos Engine Activation |

## 2. The Simplification Mandate
The Board meets every **14 days** to review operational evidence.
- **Rule**: If a workflow is repeatedly bypassed, misunderstood, or saturated, the Board MUST vote to **SIMPLIFY** or **REMOVE** the mechanism.
- **Rule**: No new features. No new semantics.

## 3. Pilot Failure Conditions
The Board is authorized to terminate the Pilot if any of the following occur:
- **Constitutional Erosion**: Emergency modes used for >30% of epochs.
- **Sociological Collapse**: Operators found using "Shadow Governance" channels.
- **Forensic Failure**: Replay determinism divergence identified by External Auditor.
- **Institutional Fatigue**: Operator MTTR (Mean Time To Respond) exceeds 48 hours for 3 consecutive incidents.

## 4. Reporting Cadence
- **Weekly Stewardship Report**: Publicly signed metrics on legitimacy health and replay stability.
- **Adversarial Drill Log**: Post-mortem of mandatory weekly drills.
