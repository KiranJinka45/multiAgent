# Trust History Asset Spec (V1.0)

## 1. Trust as a Measurable Asset
In the ZTAN ecosystem, longitudinal trust is treated as a verifiable asset. The Trust History Asset Spec defines the format and verification logic for "Trust Certificates" derived from operational history.

## 2. Reliability Index
The Reliability Index is a quantitative measure of an institution's stability over time. It is calculated from longitudinal telemetry, accounting for uptime, incident mitigation, and replay integrity.

## 3. Verifiable Certificates
Trust Certificates are cryptographically anchored and verifiable by any member of the ecosystem. They allow organizations to prove their institutional legitimacy without requiring centralized authority.

## 4. Continuity Signatures
Every certificate is backed by a "Continuity Signature," linking the current institutional state to its entire historical lineage, ensuring that trust is non-transferable and historically grounded.
