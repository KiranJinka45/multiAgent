# Longitudinal Telemetry Guide (V1.0)

## 1. Capturing Decadal History
Longitudinal Telemetry is the systematic collection and indexing of institutional operational data over long time horizons. It provides the empirical evidence for ZTAN's civilizational claims.

## 2. Stability Patterns
The `LongitudinalTelemetry` engine identifies recurring patterns of stability and resilience across the ecosystem. It correlates adversarial incidents with recovery outcomes to refine governance heuristics.

## 3. Indexing and Reconstruction
Telemetry is indexed for rapid historical reconstruction, allowing future operators or auditors to decipher ancient governance rationale even after significant evolutionary drift.

## 4. The Moat of Uptime
Ultimately, the most powerful moat for ZTAN is its accumulated record of boring, predictable uptime. Longitudinal telemetry operationalizes this record into a verifiable proof of institutional survivability.
