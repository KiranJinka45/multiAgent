# ZTAN Production Operations Manual

This document provides the operational guidelines and runbooks for maintaining ZTAN as a production-hardened civilizational substrate.

## 1. Longitudinal Trust & Reliability
- **Uptime Monitoring**: Continuous tracking of institutional uptime (Target: 99.9998%).
- **Historical Persistence**: All performance metrics must be Merkle-linked to the Governance Root to provide immutable operational evidence.

## 2. Public Transparency Reporting
- **Transparency Engine**: Automated generation of signed audit reports detailing system availability, security record, and governance integrity.
- **Machine-Verifiable Evidence**: Reports are designed to be consumed by third-party institutional auditors for autonomous validation.

## 3. High-Load Stability
- **Stress Testing**: Mandatory simulation of "black swan" high-concurrency events (100k+ concurrent root transitions).
- **Graceful Degradation**: Procedures for maintaining core institutional consensus during periods of extreme operational load.

## 4. Institutional Pilot Feedback
- **Alignment Metrics**: Systematic collection of operator behavioral data and pilot institutional feedback to ensure long-term ecosystem alignment.
