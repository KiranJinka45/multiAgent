# Independent Audit Workflows (V1.0)

## 1. The Periodic Health Audit
- **Frequency**: Quarterly.
- **Process**: Randomly sample 10% of governance events and perform full replay verification.
- **Goal**: Confirm ongoing institutional integrity.

## 2. The Emergency Containment Audit
- **Trigger**: Any circuit-breaker trip or stabilization event.
- **Process**: Immediate full reconstruction of the trigger event and the subsequent recovery sequence.
- **Goal**: Validate that emergency authority was used proportionally and constitutionally.

## 3. The Onboarding Audit
- **Trigger**: New federation joining the network.
- **Process**: Verify the initial stake deposit lineage and constitutional enrollment.
- **Goal**: Ensure new participants enter the governance structure with a clean audit trail.
