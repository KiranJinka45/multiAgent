# Evidence Lifecycle Policy (V1.0)

## 1. Economic Sustainability of Replayability
Replayability requires the preservation of evidence over long timelines. The Evidence Lifecycle Policy ensures that this preservation remains economically sustainable through tiered storage and proof compaction.

## 2. Evidence Tiers
- **Hot Evidence**: Recently generated telemetry required for immediate verification and drift detection.
- **Warm Evidence**: Compressed evidence batches stored for short-term auditing (e.g., 30-90 days).
- **Cold Evidence**: Archived evidence stored in space-efficient formats for long-term historical reconstruction (e.g., decadal timelines).

## 3. Proof Compaction
The system uses the `EvidenceLifecycle` engine to compact complex governance proofs into a space-efficient format, reducing storage costs while maintaining full cryptographic integrity.

## 4. Retention Standards
All institutions must adhere to minimum evidence retention standards to ensure the network-wide "Institutional Memory" remains intact. Pruning is only permitted for non-essential telemetry.
