# ZTAN Operational Evidence Accumulation Strategy
**Version**: 1.0.0
**Phase**: Institutional Stewardship
**Context**: Transitioning from "Declared Reliability" to "Measured Operational Reliability".

## 🎯 Strategic Objective
Accumulate a "Boring Operational History" that serves as the primary moat for the ZTAN substrate. Replace architectural claims with deterministic, machine-verifiable evidence.

## 🛠️ Priority 1: Automated Reliability Collection
Scheduled aggregation of metrics to create a continuous audit trail.
- **Tooling**: `scripts/reliability-aggregator.js` (Operationalized).
- **Target Metrics**: 
    - Replay determinism (per mission).
    - Sandbox integrity (daily).
    - Rollback success rate.
    - Economic efficiency (ROI, token cost).

## 🚀 Priority 2: First Real Pilot Missions (Constrained)
Transition from simulation to real-world impact with 100% observability.
- **Workflow**: 
    - Human-approved deployments.
    - Internal remediation tasks.
    - Sandbox-only exploration with strict resource caps.
- **Audit Requirement**: Every mission MUST generate a signed telemetry receipt.
