# Cross-Federation Continuity (V1.0)

## 1. Lineage Portability
Governance lineages in ZTAN are designed to be **Portable**. This means that Institution A can "consume" the governance history of Institution B to verify that B's current state was reached through constitutional means.

## 2. Replay Determinism
The core guarantee of cross-federation continuity is **Determinism**. Any independent auditor, regardless of their own federation, must reach the same conclusion when replaying Institution B's history.

## 3. Anchor Synchronization
Federations synchronize their "Trust Anchors" via the Global Root. This prevents "Islands of Trust" where different groups have divergent interpretations of institutional legitimacy.

## 4. Sovereignty Preservation
While trust is exchanged, **Sovereignty is Preserved**. Verifying Institution B's continuity does not grant B authority over Institution A's internal governance. It only establishes the legitimacy of B as a federated peer.
