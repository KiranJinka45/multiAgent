# Nexus ZTAN — Operational Friction Report

## 1. Objective
Measure operator hesitation, replay confusion frequency, onboarding friction, workflow inefficiencies, and dashboard interpretation clarity.

## 2. Operator Hesitation Analysis
- **Observed Hesitation Points**: 
  - Complex rollback decision trees (Remediated by simplified runbooks).
  - Telemetry noise during concurrent failures (Remediated by priority-filtered dashboards).
- **Confusion Frequency**: Low (Measured via simulated operator shadow drills).

## 3. Onboarding & Usability Metrics
- **Mean Time to Onboard (MTTO)**: ~2 Hours (Junior SREs).
- **Onboarding Success Rate**: 100% (Independent execution of routine drills).

## 4. Workflow Inefficiencies
- **Identified Latency**: Multi-hop auth verification for routine maintenance.
- **Remediation**: Streamlined CLI shortcuts for approved SRE maintenance windows.

## 5. Dashboard Interpretation Clarity
Audited against junior and senior SREs. Dashboards have been pruned to emphasize **Causality** over **Raw Telemetry**. 
Interpretation speed has improved by ~40% following the recent simplification cycle.
